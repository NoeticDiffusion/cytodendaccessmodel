## Key findings

### 1. structural_lr × replay_gain: coherent write/replay regime (70/99 = 71% pass)

A clear functional regime emerges in the upper-right of the grid (sufficient lr AND replay).
The failure region is concentrated in the lower-left (low lr, low replay_gain).
Main failure mode: SIG_A (no structural write → no overlap-branch selectivity).
This is the main article-facing heatmap.

### 2. eligibility_decay × timing_gap: temporal eligibility boundary (45/72 = 63%)

A diagonal failure boundary is visible: long timing gaps combined with fast eligibility decay
exhaust E_b before consolidation begins. Short timing gaps are robust even with fast decay.
This confirms that eligibility-based writing has a well-defined temporal window.

### 3. overlap_strength × replay_gain: overlap/replay threshold (37/72 = 51%)

Clear threshold visible: weak overlap (< ~0.40) fails even with strong replay, because
the linking metric L = Σ a_μ1 a_μ2 M_b requires non-trivial b1 weights.
Strong overlap (≥ 0.60) + sufficient replay_gain (≥ 0.40) consistently passes.

### 4. structural_decay × structural_lr: decay-vs-write trade-off (52/77 = 68%)

High decay (0.08) can be partially compensated by higher structural_lr, but the compensation
is not perfect — very high decay (0.08) still fails SIG_B even at the highest tested lr.
Main failure mode: SIG_B (linking gain insufficient when M_b decays faster than it can be written).

### 5. structural_noise × replay_gain: mostly robust (55/63 = 87% agg pass)

Noise is tolerated across most of the grid. Failures concentrate at the highest noise
level (0.08) combined with low replay_gain, where noise overwhelms the write signal.
Main failure mode: SIG_B.

### 6. context_gain × structural_lr: SIG-C architectural separation confirmed (63/77 = 82%)

SIG-C passes across the entire context_gain axis regardless of structural_lr, confirming
the E019R finding that SIG-C is an architectural fast-gating signature, not dependent on
slow structural writing. SIG-A/B/E fail only when structural_lr is near zero, regardless
of context_gain. This provides the clearest visual evidence of signature separability.
