## Key findings

### 1. Robustness confirmed for 6/9 parameters over the full tested range

`eligibility_decay`, `context_gain`, and `readout_threshold` showed **complete robustness**
across all tested values. This means the joint signature profile is insensitive to these
parameters within the tested ranges.

### 2. Two parameters show clear failure boundaries

- **`structural_lr = 0`**: SIG-A, SIG-B, SIG-E fail. When there is no learning rate, the
  slow structural write cannot occur. This is the expected and interpretable failure mode.
- **`replay_gain = 0`**: Same failure pattern. Without replay, there is no P_b build-up
  and therefore no M_b write. Also expected.

These two failures are mechanistically meaningful and support the article claim that
replay-dependent slow structural writing is necessary for the joint profile.

### 3. SIG-C is robust to context_gain variation — confirming the E018 interpretation

SIG-C passes at all 7 values of `context_gain` (0.00–3.00). This is the key E019 finding:
context separation persists even at `context_gain = 0`. SIG-C reflects the allocation
structure (which branches mu_alpha and mu_beta engage), not dynamic M_b writing.

**Article implication:** SIG-C is an architectural fast-gating signature.

### 4. Timing gap: robustness up to 12 steps

SIG-B fails at 16 steps, SIG-A at 24 steps. This means the model can tolerate a moderate
delay between encoding and consolidation while still showing structural writing. The 9 null
steps of damage do not themselves cause failure in the main canonical protocol (damage is
a separate phase with an explicit decay rate; timing_gap only applies pre-consolidation).

### 5. structural_noise: 4/7 values fully pass (all seeds), moderate noise tolerated

Values 0.00–0.02 show 100% seed pass rate. At 0.035 and above, some seeds fail, and at
0.05–0.08, >50% of seeds may fail. The canonical parameter is structural_noise=0.00,
so this is a valid stability check, not a claim about noise robustness.

### 6. SIG-E canonical value discrepancy vs E018

The standalone `_run_single` canonical SIG-E = +154.7 pp vs E018's +33.3 pp. This difference
arises because targeted rescue with 3 rounds × 3 cues × 3 consolidation passes heavily
rebuilds M_b on b1 (targeted pre-cueing builds strong E_b), causing L to overshoot the
"healthy" reference. `_recovery_pct > 100%` is possible. The generic baseline (plain
consolidation) recovers much less, yielding the large positive difference.

This does not invalidate SIG-E — both E018 and E019 agree on the direction (targeted rescue
> plain consolidation). The magnitude reflects how aggressively the targeted protocol
rebuilds the overlap branch. This should be noted in the methods section.
